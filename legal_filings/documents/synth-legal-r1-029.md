# STIPULATION OF DISMISSAL

Sterling Capital L.P., Plaintiff-Appellee, v. Crestline Logistics L.P., Defendant-Appellant. No. 16-2302. United States Court of Appeals, Second Circuit. Nov. 30, 1979. Argued and submitted Oct. 10, 1979; decided Nov. 30, 1979. Reported at 495 F.2d 313. Ruth Jackson, for the plaintiff; Paul Bybee, for the defendant. Before KILKENNY, FLETCHER and FERGUSON, Circuit Judges. The matter had earlier been referred to Magistrate Judge William Gonzalez for a report and recommendation, which the court now adopts in part. This stipulation comes before United States Court of Appeals, Second Circuit on the pleadings filed of record. Comes now the plaintiff, Sterling Capital L.P., and respectfully states as follows. The court, having reviewed the submissions in case number No. 16-2302, and being fully advised, enters the following. WHEREFORE, for the foregoing reasons, the relief requested is addressed herein. Dated November 30, 1979.
