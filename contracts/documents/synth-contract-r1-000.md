# AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

This Agreement and Plan of Merger (this “Agreement”), is made and entered into as of February 13, 2018, by and between Granite Peak Industries LLC (the “Parent”), and Beacon Hill Manufacturing Inc. (the “Company”). Although executed and delivered by the parties on March 3, 2018, this Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated November 8, 2017 setting forth the general terms of their proposed arrangement; and WHEREAS, the parties now desire to set forth the definitive terms and conditions governing their relationship as Parent and Company; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions hereof, shall continue for an initial term of approximately 7 year(s), expiring on February 11, 2025.

2. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the parties agree that any action to enforce an arbitral award may, for the convenience of the parties, be brought in the courts of the State of Minnesota.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
