# SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

This Settlement Agreement and Release (this “Agreement”), is made and entered into as of November 15, 2019, by and between Pinnacle Inc. (the “Releasor”), and Vanguard Manufacturing LLC (the “Releasee”). Although executed and delivered by the parties on November 17, 2019, this Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated September 9, 2019 setting forth the general terms of their proposed arrangement; and WHEREAS, the parties now desire to set forth the definitive terms and conditions governing their relationship as Releasor and Releasee; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in full force and effect until terminated by either party upon not less than 60 days’ prior written notice to the other party.

2. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Maryland, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the parties agree that any action to enforce an arbitral award may, for the convenience of the parties, be brought in the courts of the State of Michigan.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
