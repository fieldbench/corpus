# REVOLVING CREDIT AGREEMENT

This Credit Agreement (this “Agreement”), is made and entered into as of June 13, 2015, by and between Granite Peak Energy LLC (the “Borrower”), and Copperline Materials Inc. (the “Lender”). Although executed and delivered by the parties on June 17, 2015, this Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated May 1, 2015 setting forth the general terms of their proposed arrangement; and WHEREAS, the parties now desire to set forth the definitive terms and conditions governing their relationship as Borrower and Lender; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in full force and effect until terminated by either party upon not less than 30 days’ prior written notice to the other party.

2. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the parties agree that any action to enforce an arbitral award may, for the convenience of the parties, be brought in the courts of the State of Colorado.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
