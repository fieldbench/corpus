# SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this “Agreement”), is made and entered into as of November 15, 2022, by and between Crestwood Networks L.P. (the “Service Provider”), and Stonebridge Industries Corporation (the “Client”). Although executed and delivered by the parties on November 30, 2022, this Agreement shall be effective as of the date first written above.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated August 31, 2022 setting forth the general terms of their proposed arrangement; and WHEREAS, the parties now desire to set forth the definitive terms and conditions governing their relationship as Service Provider and Client; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in full force and effect until terminated by either party upon not less than 30 days’ prior written notice to the other party.

2. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the parties agree that any action to enforce an arbitral award may, for the convenience of the parties, be brought in the courts of the State of Georgia.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date first written above.
